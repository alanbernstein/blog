alternative: cheaper plywood instead of the nicely finished maple stuff.
alternative: #8 x 1/2" wood screw (100x), instead of 8-32 screws+nuts+washers. I prefer using nuts rather than trusting the 1/2" screws in 3/8" of plywood.
alternative: vinyl tubing, instead of heat shrink. Much more work to install onto the bolts.
